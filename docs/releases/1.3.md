# SirisLearn 1.3

Two built-in AI courses, for staying ahead on AI at work.

- **How AI works** (intermediate, 8 lessons): what AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI are; how models learn; neural networks and embeddings; transformers and LLMs; what AI is good and bad at; multimodal models, tools, RAG and agents; evaluating AI properly; and risks, ethics and regulation with an Australian focus (Privacy Act, AI Ethics Principles, government AI adoption guidance, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act).
- **Leading with AI in engineering consulting** (advanced, 8 lessons): prompting that works; checking AI output; where AI helps across bids, reports, reviews and scripts; handling company and client information; automation and agents; AI governance in a consultancy; choosing use cases and proving value; and being the AI leader in your firm, with a 90-day plan.
- Each lesson has a diagram, key points and ten questions; final exams, daily review and fresh retakes work as for any course.
- They're added to the library on the next start. AI rules and products change quickly: the regulation lessons say to check the current position, and with a model connected you can create a fresh course on any newer AI topic.
