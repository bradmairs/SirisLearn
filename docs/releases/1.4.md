# SirisLearn 1.4

Practice that sticks, and courses that stay current.

- **Calculation questions.** Enter a number (with its unit shown); it's right within the question's tolerance, and the worked solution appears after. The right digits at the wrong power of ten count as partly right, with a nudge to check your units. New lessons get one or two where they teach a calculation, and the water and AI courses have new ones (head loss, pump power, affinity laws, Manning, Joukowsky, wet well storage, recall, a business case).
- **Scenario questions.** A short real-world case ("odour at a rising main discharge", "a pump station alarm at 2 am", "fabricated references in a graduate's report") marked against a rubric. Intermediate and advanced lessons get one; the water and AI courses have five new ones.
- **Better feedback on written answers.** You see which key points (or rubric items) you covered and which you missed. Didn't get a written or calculation answer right? You get a hint, then **Try again** once for half marks or **Show answer** (lesson quizzes and practice; not exams). The answer stays hidden until you've used or given up the second try, and the review card keeps your first answer.
- **Weak spots.** A new screen with your accuracy by kind of question and the lessons you're getting wrong, with **Practise** to drill one (missed questions first). Your top weak spot shows on Today, and the daily review serves weak spots first when more cards are due than fit.
- **Learning paths.** Chain courses towards a goal with an optional target date: progress, whether you're on track and how many steps a week it takes. *AI leader* (How AI works, then Leading with AI in engineering consulting) is added for you. Paths show on Today and in SirisOS 9.3 (with an inbox nudge when one falls behind).
- **Lessons that stay current.** Lessons about things that change quickly are flagged *may be out of date* after 6 months, and **Bring up to date** rewrites one from its old version (plus recent AI news for AI courses) with a fresh quiz.
- **What's new in AI.** Each month, a new lesson in the course "What's new in AI", written from AI news feeds with its sources. Turn it off or make this month's now in Settings. Needs a model.
- **Built-in courses upgrade in place.** The water and AI courses go to version 2 with the new questions; your progress and review cards are kept.
- New settings: `SIRISLEARN_REFRESH_DAYS`, `SIRISLEARN_AI_UPDATES`, `SIRISLEARN_AI_UPDATE_DAY`, `SIRISLEARN_AI_FEEDS`, `SIRISLEARN_INSIGHT_DAYS`. New dependency: `defusedxml`.
