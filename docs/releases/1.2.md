# SirisLearn 1.2

Nine built-in courses, ready to learn from without a model.

- **Docker**: *Docker from scratch*, *Docker in practice* and *Docker at expert level*.
- **Linux**: *Linux from scratch*, *Linux power user* and *Linux internals and administration*.
- **Water and civil engineering** (Victorian and Australian context): *Water and civil engineering fundamentals*, *Water engineering design* and *Water engineering at expert level*.
- Each course has two sections of three lessons; every lesson has a diagram, key points and ten questions (multiple choice and open, at least two on pictures). Final exams, daily review and fresh retakes work with them like any other course.
- The courses are added to the library once on the next start (`SIRISLEARN_SEED_PACKS=false` turns this off).
- Right answers in built-in courses are spread evenly across A–D.
- Lessons can now show Markdown tables.
- A test checks every built-in lesson against the same cleaning and rules as a generated one.
