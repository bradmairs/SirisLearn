# SirisLearn 1.1

Making learning stick:
- **Daily review**: every question you answer joins a spaced-repetition queue (the Leitner system). Right answers come back after 3, 7, 14, 30 then 60 days; wrong ones tomorrow. Review from Today; it counts for your streak.
- **Fresh questions on retakes**: after each quiz, a new set is written in the background, so retakes test understanding rather than memory. Without a model, seen questions come back with shuffled options.
- **Streaks count passed quizzes** (or a daily review, or a passed exam), not failed ones.
- **Streak freezes**: earn one every 7 days (hold up to 2); a single missed day uses one automatically.

Siris:
- Passed lessons are **saved to your Second Brain** (through SirisAI), once each.
- **Topic ideas** on Today come from your Second Brain's busiest topics.
- **Ask Siris** about any lesson, or **Help me understand** on a wrong answer; *Continue in Siris* hands off to the SirisOS assistant.
- **Listen** reads a lesson aloud with your device's voice.

Like LinkedIn Learning:
- **Final exam** per course (20 questions from every lesson, 80% to pass) and a printable **certificate**.
- **Photos** from Wikimedia Commons in lessons, with their credit.
- Courses get an **area and tags**: the Library filters by area, shows a **course map**, and courses list related ones.

Polish:

- **Phone notifications**: an evening streak reminder and a morning review reminder (turn on per device in Settings).
- History labels days by the app's time zone, so "Today" is always right.
- Settings shows what the course writer has **cost**, by course and by use.
- **Daily database backups** in `data/backups` (14 kept).
- Six new awards (reviews, long memory, exams, freezes).
